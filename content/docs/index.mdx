FORGE runs fleets of physical machines and bills for the hours they work. Stake
an asset into the pool and you earn a share of what those hours are billed at.

<Split>
  <Side title="Stakers" hue="aqua">
    <p>Deposit stablecoins, blue-chip crypto or tokenised assets. Earn from
    machine-hours that have been billed and collected.</p>
  </Side>
  <Side title="Contributors" hue="copper">
    <p>Record the robotics data the machines are trained on. Every verified task
    mints a receipt onchain.</p>
  </Side>
</Split>

The rate is a share of billed hours, so it rises when the fleet is busy and
falls when it is quiet.

## What you can stake

| Group | Assets |
| --- | --- |
| Stablecoins | USDC, USDT |
| Blue-chip crypto | ETH, WBTC |
| Tokenised equities | Held as themselves, so the position carries the equity's price as well as the yield |
| Tokenised commodities | Same, for metals and energy |

What you stake is what the position holds.

## The data programme

Machines have to be trained, and robotics training data has to be recorded by
people doing physical things: moving the mug, saying the sentence, photographing
the shelf.

FORGE runs a [task programme](/docs/tasks) for exactly that. Contribution is
scored in [Copper](/docs/copper).

<Callout title="Read this before planning around Copper" type="warn">
Copper is a record of contribution and a **potential future reward**. Rates and
season terms are decided when a season closes and are not published in advance.
</Callout>
