## What you can stake

| Group | Assets | What the position holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stablecoins | USDC, USDT | Dollars. The yield is a dollar yield |
| Blue-chip crypto | ETH, WBTC | The asset itself, so its price moves your position too |
| Tokenised equities | Held as themselves | The equity's price, plus the yield |
| Tokenised commodities | Metals and energy | The commodity's price, plus the yield |

Every asset does the same job in the pool.

## How the rate is set

Revenue collected from billed machine-hours, divided by what was staked over
the same period. A trailing figure, published after the fact.

The app shows the rate each asset actually earned.

## Withdrawals

Capital that is not currently backing a booked machine can leave straight away.
Capital that is cannot: it is released as those hours are worked and collected.

So withdrawals run through a queue, and the estimate you are shown is built from
the current book rather than a nominal number.

<Callout title="Why there is a queue at all" type="warn">
A pool that promised instant exit while its capital sat behind month-long
bookings would be promising something it cannot always do. The queue is the
alternative to discovering that during the one week everybody asks at once.
</Callout>

## What staking also earns

Positions accrue [Copper](/docs/copper) for as long as they are staked. Copper
is a contribution score and a potential future reward. It is not interest, not a
token, and it is not part of the rate quoted above.
