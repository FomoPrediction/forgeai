## Supply

**1,000,000,000 FORGE.** Fixed at deployment, with no mint function. Nothing in
the protocol can create a token after launch, so every number below is a share
of a total that cannot move.

Emissions are not a lever here. A data market that pays contributors by printing
more of its own token is paying them with the value of the tokens they already
hold, and the second season costs the first one.

## Allocation

<Callout title="Why contributors take the largest share">
The protocol has nothing to sell that contributors did not record. An allocation

it is describing a different business.

### Contributor rewards · 42% · 420,000,000 FORGE

Paid to the people who do the tasks. Released season by season against work that
has already been verified onchain, so tokens follow data rather than promises.

No cliff and no schedule. A season opens, work is done, the season closes and
its pool converts. Nothing is released for a season nobody worked.

### Ecosystem and liquidity · 18% · 180,000,000 FORGE

Exchange liquidity, integrations, and the parts of the network that will not
start on their own: new task tracks, checkers for hardware nobody owns yet, and
the first buyers of a dataset that does not exist until somebody records it.

A quarter at launch for liquidity. The rest over 36 months, spent against
specific programmes rather than drip fed.

### Core contributors · 17.5% · 175,000,000 FORGE

The people building and running the protocol, present and future hires.

Nothing for 12 months. A quarter at month 12, then equal monthly amounts across
the following 36 months. Four years to full.

### Investors · 14% · 140,000,000 FORGE

Early backers who funded the contracts, the checkers and the first season of
rewards before there was any revenue.

Nothing for 12 months. A quarter at month 12, then equal monthly amounts across
the following 24 months.

### Treasury reserve · 8.5% · 85,000,000 FORGE

Held against things that have not happened yet: an audit finding worth paying to
fix, a dataset worth buying outright, a shortfall the staking module cannot
cover.

Streams evenly over 48 months. Spending it needs a governance vote, so the
schedule is a ceiling and not a plan.

## Unlocks

Core contributors and investors are on the same shape and the same cliff:
nothing for twelve months, a quarter at month 12, the rest monthly. The team
streams over 36 months after that and investors over 24, so the people running
the protocol are the last to finish.

## Contributor seasons

[Copper](/docs/copper) is the score contributors earn today, before FORGE
exists. It is not a token, it does not transfer, and it is not a claim on
anything. It is a record of verified work.

The contributor allocation is released in seasons. A season opens, work is done,
the season closes, and the Copper earned in it becomes a **potential reward**
against that season's pool.

<Callout title="Season terms are published when a season opens" type="warn">
Pool sizes, conversion rates and eligibility are not announced in advance and
are not listed here.

A published rate is a target. The moment the arithmetic is public, the cheapest
route to a bigger number becomes the strategy, and the dataset the protocol
exists to produce gets worse. What is fixed and stated is the share of supply
contributors are paid from; how a given season divides it is decided when that
season closes.
</Callout>

Nothing here is a promise of a payment. Copper is a measure of contribution, and
a season converts it only on the terms set for that season.

## What FORGE is not

FORGE is not equity, not a share of revenue, and not a claim on treasury assets.
Holding it does not entitle anyone to a payment. What it does is set the
parameters the protocol runs on, which the [token page](/docs/token) lists in
full.

Receipts are separate and always will be. A receipt is a soulbound record that a
specific person did a specific task; it cannot be sold, and buying FORGE never
produces one.
