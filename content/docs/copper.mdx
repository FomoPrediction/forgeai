## What Copper is

A ledger of verified work. It does not transfer, it is not a token, and holding
it is not a claim on anything. It exists so that contribution is measured from
the first day rather than from whenever a token launches.

The ledger is append only. A balance is the sum of a wallet's rows, never a
stored number, so a wrong award is corrected by writing its opposite and both
entries stay visible.

## How it is earned

| Source | When it pays |
| --- | --- |
| Verified tasks | On mint. Pass a task's check, mint its receipt, and Copper lands with it |
| Staking | Continuously, alongside machine-hour yield, for as long as the asset stays staked |
| Referrals | When somebody you referred passes a task or stakes. Both count, and both keep paying |
| Daily streak | A day counts once you clear that day's task requirement |

<Callout title="Rates are not published">
What a task pays is not a fixed number and is not listed anywhere. It varies
with difficulty and with the quality of what was submitted, and it is adjusted
between seasons.

This is deliberate. A published rate is a target: the moment a number is public,
the cheapest way to raise it becomes the strategy, and the data gets worse. Your
own balance and history are always visible in the app.
</Callout>

## Tiers

Four tiers, earned by contribution. Each one raises what you can do rather than
paying you more directly.

| Tier | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Ember | Where everyone starts. Base submission limit, base yield |
| Anvil | A higher daily submission limit, a yield boost, early access to new tracks |
| Forge | Higher again, a larger yield boost, priority in the simulator queue |
| Crucible | No submission limit, the largest yield boost, a vote on which tracks ship next |

Thresholds move between seasons and are not published. The app shows your
current tier and how far along you are toward the next one, which is the part
that is useful to you; the exact number is the part that is only useful to
somebody optimising against it.

Tiers are earned by contribution, never by deposit size. A large depositor who
has never completed a task sits at Ember.

## Streak

Clear the day's task requirement and the day counts. Miss a day and the count
returns to zero, but anything a streak has already unlocked stays yours.

The requirement is a whole number of verified tasks, not a proportion: falling
one short is the same as doing none that day.
